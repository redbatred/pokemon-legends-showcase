About this animation test

Normal playback waits for your Throw Poké Ball press; timing does not affect the selected mock outcome. Recording mode demonstrates the press after the guide’s spoken prompt. Water and cavern light continue moving during the wait. This scene starts after the third bonus symbol has arrived. The sequential walkthrough uses the actual normal pack stage and enters this scene on collection. The reward is led by a physical card flip, a fixed multiplier badge and a separate five-step amount tally. Brief Spine cut-ins stay in the scene margins, and capture and counting shots have no character overlay; a frustrated reaction follows the completed reward.
