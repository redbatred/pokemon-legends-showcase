# V45 unified finisher, return and Charizard shading

- Keep Tera crowns physical/crystalline; return character skin to textured cel shading so Charizard no longer has a clear-coated faceted ceramic body.
- Shared final strike: source attack, count endpoint, complete-glyph number recoil, authored impact cels, camera and layered audio meet at one contact time. Reserve a short held strike and then recover into a readable total.
- Return: retract camera and award under an authored cel sweep; restore normal stage underneath and carry the existing amount into its HUD slot once. Avoid duplicate source/destination actors and cut tails.
- Performance: measure the same scene before/after; eliminate the extra 2D copy of the 3D canvas, skip hidden Spine updates and cache static choreography/geometry work. Preserve dynamic texture resizing.
- Verify skip/advance/credit-once, reduced motion, exit timing, material retention, full build/tests, phone scene and recording with live sound.
