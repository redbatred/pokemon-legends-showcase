# V53 — smoother scene handoff, preparation and spectator motion

## Diagnosed stalls

The matched V52-code baseline had a 111 ms maximum wall-clock frame gap and a 61 ms cinema render at the first visible court impact. The effect was invisible during compile/warm-up. Next-Pokémon preparation ran around cinematic age 3 seconds. A later instrumented run measured 44 ms in cinema teardown.

- Activate the transient court Spine mesh during asynchronous shader compilation and warm a frame while the mesh is visible.
- Wait for the current attack recovery before preparing the next Pokémon; reset releases pending waiters and stale game-phase callbacks cannot prepare an old pack.
- Sample model camera bounds in 1,024-vertex chunks with a 3 ms yield budget, preserving all original sampled vertices and poses.
- Reuse a bounded pool of idle WebGL rendering contexts. Active scenes have independent canvases; teardown drains the pool and late releases from older generations are disposed.

## Motion

- 48 small spectator groups use slower clap/wave/held-cheer cycles with offset timing. Four illustrated poses blend with normalized weights; the final cheer blends continuously from the current pose. Small upper-body sway is anchored at row boundaries; the stands and aisles stay still.

- Extend return to 820 ms, gradually pull the camera back, clear the arena through the gusts, then transfer the amount after the curtain reaches its center. The original actor remains hidden until the card handoff completes.

## Continuous source attack and silent monitoring

The source reveal and win transition were both advancing the same model. `winOrigins` restarted `charge` at age 0.42, even though the reveal had already reached a later pose. The transition now takes the current attack clock and advances monotonically to its release beat. The previous reveal timeline stops writing attack poses and no longer starts a second release/recovery while the transition owns the actor.

Local speaker monitoring can be disconnected independently from the capture destination. V53 test/recording pages mute that route before interaction. Exported videos retain the stereo Web Audio mix; testing does not play through the speakers.
