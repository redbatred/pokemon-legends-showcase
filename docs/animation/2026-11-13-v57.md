# V57 — championship finisher

The previous change kept the same staging and was rejected as too small. This pass changes the environment, shot structure, impact and typography.

- Enclosed championship set with physical curved trusses, instanced ribs and floodlights, animated light movement, and an arena emblem. Existing crowd plates are retained within the seating area; the old outdoor skyline is removed.
- The native animated head drives a genuine close-up during final charge (5.12–5.98s). That shot ends before impact so its lens cannot crop the award.
- Existing authored fire/water/etc. cels play through Spine across the screen at contact, then separate and clear by 6.64s. A skip suppresses this takeover.
- Battle lettering uses Bangers from the Google Fonts repository under the bundled OFL. Amounts use a separate bitmap font; normal game typography remains unchanged. During return the amount crossfades into the normal HUD lettering.
- Final layout moves into its victory position after contact. The animation remains reusable across the existing Pokémon model, move and tier data.

Validation: an intermediate full suite passed 201 files / 1,022 tests. After final close-up and return refinements, 39 focused tests across seven files passed; the production build passed. Two overlapping broad rechecks were stopped and replaced by that focused run. No claim is made that a final full-suite rerun completed.

Live capture remains a limitation: two runs showed sustained frame stalls, and showing the browser did not resolve them. The published artifact is explicitly a deterministic animation preview, not live-performance evidence. It renders the actual WinCinema component at 60 samples per second from scene time 3.0 through 8.483 seconds (330 frames at 1080×1440). It shows Charizard, not the full pack flow. Sound is mixed from the same existing game-cue assets at the animation event timestamps. Local testing remained muted. The export is 5.5 seconds with stereo AAC.
