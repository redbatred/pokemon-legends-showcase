# Pack-total win and plain card wins — light pass (2026-11-14)

Scope: the award shown when all cards are laid out after a pack (`PackWinCinema`, `ResultWin`) and the plain per-card
award under the card (`RewardTransfers`, non-arena path). Presentation only: no outcome, ledger or credit rule changed.

## What changed

**Shared light (`AwardJuice.ts`, new).** A small pooled system built on `juiceTextures()`: pressure rings (round or laid
on a floor), flare, rays, sparks, shed motes, drawn-in streaks and a shower of shaded coins, plus `LightComet` (hot head,
tail along the path). `awardPower(amount, bet)` scales a beat by the award against the stake (a few cents ≈ 0.3, the
stake itself = 1). `ELEMENT_LIGHT` gives each element its light colour. Headless runs keep the layers empty.

**Pack total ≥ 10× (`PackWinCinema`).** Removed: the anime cel Spine attack frames (`loadWinAttack('electric'|'wind')`),
`animeCelMatte`, the flat full-brightness painted backdrop and the Nunito italic title. Now one chain:

1. The arena is a dim room; the light in it lives at the amount (a pool that warms with the count, slow rays behind it,
   spill on the hand of cards).
2. Each paying card lifts (smaller than before, so it no longer covers the amount), light is drawn into it, its figure
   (`+$15.00`, battle face) sits on the card.
3. The value leaves as a comet that accelerates into the amount and lands exactly on the card's existing beat
   (`packCometWindow`); the amount takes a ring, flare and sparks, with a `win-tick` that rises in pitch card by card.
4. Before the last card lands the room inhales (streaks drawn into the amount, rays dim).
5. The slam: a 75 ms hit-stop (`packFinaleClock`), a white impact frame, two shock rings and rays, a wave that pushes
   each card away from the amount in distance order (`packRipple`), the tier stamped letter by letter in the battle face
   with `TIER_PALETTE` colours, and a coin shower. Sounds: `win-final-N`, `win-coins-N`, `win-stamp`.
6. Tap behaviour is unchanged: first tap jumps to the slam (which still plays), second tap exits into the receipt.

Tiers are presentational: 10× BIG, 50× MEGA, 100× EPIC (`packWinTier`), same labels as before.

**Receipt / small totals (`ResultWin`).** Removed: `WinArtwork` winged ornaments and the Spine confetti/fountain rig
(`preloadResultWin` is gone). Now: a pool of light under the amount warms as the count climbs, each count step is a
`win-tick` a little higher with a spark, and the landing is one pop sized by the win against its stake; from BIG up it
adds rays and coins behind the amount. From BIG up the title wears the battle face and tier colours, so the cinema's
title and the receipt's title are the same object visually at the hand-off.

**Plain card award (`RewardTransfers`, tier `null`; flight also for `special`).** The `+$6.00` drops onto the stone from
above its size and rings out once (ring on the pedestal, flare, sparks, a quiet pitched tick), rests on a faint pool of
its card's colour, then leaves as a comet that swings round the card (alternating sides) with shed motes instead of the
`Graphics` line trail. Arrival: flare/ring/sparks on TOTAL WIN, and the total itself stretches harder the more the award
was worth (`MoneyReadout.pulse(strength)`, driven by `RewardTransfers.arrivalPower`). Everything scales with
`awardPower`: `$0.25` on a `$10` pack is a thin ring and nothing else. The arena (cinematic) branch keeps its own trail
and timing; only its arrival burst on the total now uses the same light instead of eight drawn lines.

## Layout
`packFinaleGeometry` gained `liftY`/`liftWidth`, a taller pre-exit title, a larger amount on narrow portrait screens
and a fan that is limited by height (short landscape no longer buries the prompt). `packCinemaLayout.titleSize` is a
little smaller so the battle-face title clears the receipt's divider.

## Verified (contact sheets and full-size stills on the virtual clock)
- Cinema: 540×720 (BIG, 3 and 10 paying cards), 390×844 (MEGA), 800×571 (EPIC), 1280×720 (BIG), incl. tap-to-skip and
  the exit into the receipt.
- Result count without cinema: small total at 540×720; ≥10× total under turbo at 540×720.

## Known, outside this pass
While the stage's own ticker is stopped (results screen, pack cinema) `Stage.draw()` coalesces renders onto the *next*
animation frame, and the GSAP tick that asks for them runs before it in that frame — measured in the lab as a render on
every other frame (30 fps). Rendering in a microtask (or a lowest-priority GSAP ticker listener) in `Stage.draw` would
give these screens the full frame rate.

Now unused by game code: `WinArtwork.ts`, `resultWinRigData.ts` (+ test). Lab helper: `tests/browser/pw-helpers.js`.

## Second pass: no rings, connected light, a designed panel

Feedback on the first pass: the same white circle answered every beat, effects did not connect, and the modal itself needed work. This pass removes every ring, stroked circle and round pool of light from the pack result, the pack cinema and the plain card award, and rebuilds them around one rule: light has a source and a destination, and you can watch it travel.

### What replaced the ring

`AwardJuice` no longer has `ring` or `pop`. A hit is now `strike(x,y,width,tint,power,angle)`:

- an **anamorphic lens streak** across what was struck (`flare`, a new texture in `awardTextures()`: bright in the middle, tapering to both ends, with a hot hairline);
- **sparks thrown the way the light was travelling** (`sparks` takes an angle and a spread);
- the target itself reacts: the amount pulses and, in the cinema, spreads and flattens for an instant (`squashX`/`squashY` in `packScoreMotion`).

The pool of light under an amount became a thin **bar of light** the digits stand on; it lengthens as the count climbs. `glint` is a small four-point glint for letters.

### Receipt (the PACK COMPLETE modal)

- **The panel** (`resultPanelArt.ts`, shared with the cinema's exit so they cross-fade into the same object): a raised slab with a contact shadow, light falling from above, a lit header with a gold rule and a red key, a recessed plate the title and total sit in, and a footer ledge for the buttons. Shapes only.
- **Opening** (`CanvasUI.animateResults`): the panel comes up with weight and settles; the cards are dealt from the tray in order with a quarter turn, land, give slightly and take a contact shadow; each value appears under its card; the buttons arrive last. The drawn cyan trail lines are gone.
- **The count is paid for** (`ResultWin.payLight`): the total holds at $0.00 until the cards are on the table, then each paying card in turn sends a streak of its own colour to the total. As the light leaves, that card's value lights up and pops; as it lands, the total steps by exactly that value with a lens streak, sparks along the direction of travel and a tick a step higher than the last. The total always shows the sum of what has been paid. Tap lands it at once.
- From BIG up, rays open behind the total and the payout spills from behind the title and comes to rest on the plate's lip.
- Reduced motion and turbo keep the previous plain count (turbo with directional sparks instead of rings).

### Pack cinema (totals of 10× and up)

- **Per card:** the card lifts; a foil sweep crosses its artwork and runs off its edge, and the streak is born at that edge; the card dims once its light has left; the figure rides the streak; the amount takes a lens streak and sparks carried past it; the rest of the hand hops once, card to card outward from the middle (`packHop`).
- **Room:** dim, and it warms as the total grows; faint rays grow behind the amount with the total, close for the breath before the slam and open on it. No halo, no floor disc.
- **Finale:** one foil sweep crosses the whole hand left to right (`packSweep`); as it runs off each card that card's light leaves for the amount with a rising tick, and the last streak lands exactly on the slam. The slam is a lens streak the width of the room, sparks thrown out along it, a white frame, the hit-stop, the hand pushed back in a wave, and layers shifting against each other. The tier's letters come in along the streak from either side and lock with a glint. Coins spill from behind the tier and come to rest on the bar under the total.

### Plain card award

The `+$6.00` strikes a lens streak along the stone's edge with sparks to both sides and sits on a bar of light; its arrival on TOTAL WIN is a streak across the total with sparks carried on in the direction it flew.

### 30 fps on the result screens

`Stage.draw()` used to schedule its render on the next animation frame, after the GSAP tick that asked for it, so every other frame was skipped while the ticker was stopped. It now renders in a microtask at the end of the callback that asked. Measured in the lab with `pwRenderRate()`: 30 of 30 frames render on the result screen (15 of 30 before).

### Checked as stills

Cinema and receipt at 540×720, 390×844 and 1280×720 (BIG, 10 paying cards); paid count in the receipt at 540×720 and 800×571, including tap-to-land mid-count; plain award at 800×571. No text overlaps and no circles in any of them. Not reviewed at real speed or with sound. Not looked at: MEGA/EPIC palettes this pass, the cinema at 800×571, session and interlude result modes (same component, no cards to pay from), and low-detail mode.

## Third pass: the bonus in the modal, and a count as short as a slot's

### The bonus win is part of the pack
`packTiles(pack)` (in `packResultsLayout.ts`) is a settled pack's cards followed by `bonusCard(pack.bonus)`. The result modal, the history rows ("10 CARDS + BONUS", best-card thumbnails), the pack replay fan and the result launch all read it.
- **Its tile:** gold double edge on a dark gold ground, `BONUS 180×` in the battle face across the foot of the reward card, its amount under it in gold. It is the last tile, so it pays last.
- **Arriving on an open modal** (the bonus played after Open All): the modal remembers the slots it last drew for that pack. The other tiles slide to make room, the bonus tile drops in from the middle of the screen and settles, only it pays, and the total rolls on from the old total to the new one with every tier it crosses. Nothing else restarts.

### One count, slot length
The 10×+ "cinema" (`PackWinCinema.ts`, `packCinemaLayout.ts`) is gone, and with it the 6.12 s it borrowed from the arena win. Every total now runs the same count in the modal (`ResultWin` + `packFinaleMotion.ts`):
1. **Cascade.** As each tile comes to rest it sends its light to the total, 40 ms after the one before. The roll starts when the first light lands.
2. **Roll.** `packCountPlan(from,to,price,start)`: 0.85 s, plus 0.42 s for each tier crossed. Each segment accelerates into its threshold, catches there for 0.11 s while the name upgrades in place (BIG → MEGA → EPIC → LEGENDARY at 10/50/100/150× the pack price) with a punch, a lens streak, sparks and a `win-rung` cue, then moves on. Ticks rise in pitch and come faster to the end.
3. **Takeover.** A total that reaches a tier dims the rest of the screen for its roll and grows; the dim lifts as soon as it has landed.
4. **Landing.** Hit-stop (70 ms), a white frame, the digits punch and turn gold, the panel gives once and the tiles hop outward from the total, coins spill from behind the name onto the plate's lip. Then it holds.

Measured from the modal appearing, cards dealt from the tray (540×720):

| Total | Tiers crossed | Lands at |
|---|---|---|
| $15.15 on a $10 pack | none | 1.72 s |
| $133 (BIG) | 1 | 2.15 s |
| MEGA | 2 | 2.59 s (model) |
| EPIC | 3 | 3.01 s (model) |
| $1,815.15 (LEGENDARY, with bonus) | 4 | 3.42 s |
| late bonus, $15.15 → $1,815.15 | 4 | 3.27 s after it arrives |

One tap (on the total, on Continue, or anywhere that is not a control while it counts) lands it at once; the next continues. Turbo runs the same count 2.4× faster; reduced motion shows the settled total.

### Digits
`ResultDigits.ts` is the modal's own readout (`MoneyReadout` is untouched): each glyph is painted once per size into an ink texture and a face texture, and a number is a fixed set of sprites. Rolling swaps textures and moves sprites; no text is laid out or rasterised during a count, and the face tint goes from cream to gold on the landing.

### Fit
Tested in `packFinaleMotion.test.ts` for 11 and 12 tiles at 360×640, 390×844, 540×720, 768×1024, 800×571, 844×390, 1280×720 and 1920×1080: every tile inside the panel, below the plate, its caption above the footer, no two tiles touching, buttons inside the footer. Changes: the short landscape layout takes 12 across; from 1500×900 up the panel grows to 1180×880 and its cards to 164 wide; on a short plate the takeover does not lift the name into the header.

### Performance
- **Idle modal:** it used to render 60 frames a second for as long as it was open whenever a special card was in the grid (the animated frame on its thumbnail asked for a redraw every tick). Thumbnails are now static and the count stops asking for frames 1.5 s after it lands: 0 renders a second at rest (measured, 60 → 0).
- **During the count:** the total, its light and its digits are their own render groups with fixed children, so the modal's display list is not rebuilt while it counts (measured over a LEGENDARY roll: 1 rebuild when the bonus tile is added, 0 during the roll and landing). Glyph textures are drawn once, unseen, before the roll. The cinema's per-frame work is gone: 11 perspective meshes each with a foil filter, a full-screen `Graphics` redrawn every frame, per-frame text restyling and a new hit rectangle every frame.
- Wall-clock cost per frame in the lab swings between 0.3 ms and 20 ms from run to run with the tab in the background, so it is not quoted as a before/after.
