# V47 physically placed pressure effects

The V46 background flame loops had no physical source and appeared to burn the grandstands. Removed all vertical/background elemental billboards and the perpetual ground-wave clock. Stadium reactions now follow an actual strike: a finite court-level Spine pressure burst, a bounded light wave and delayed flag response. The hold returns to the living stadium/camera/actor without replaying attacks or effects.

The normal release used the raw wind spritesheet without the matte cleanup elsewhere. Its low-alpha rectangular cell residue was enlarged around the whole body. Replaced that usage with an original vector-art atlas (`pressure.svg`) and a 14-slot Spine 4.2 rig. Individual pressure arcs emanate from the actual ball aperture. Independently moving dust curls start at foot contact for grounded species only. No full-body billboard, bitmap edits, or generated raster art.

The new stadium pressure rig combines all Spine slot geometry in one depth-tested draw call on the court. Radius is bounded well inside the grandstand. Existing smooth motion, return barrier, intact card restore and outcome/credit logic are preserved.
