# V56 — final-strike victory composition

The requested direction is a substantially larger, more emphatic normal special win, informed by real slot animation references. Mock payouts and settlement remain unchanged.

## Reference study

- Artforgame, Trees of Fortune: https://artforgame.com/portfolio-item/trees-of-fortune/ . Inspected the creator's Big Win and Super Win clips with the browser muted. Useful principles: darken the surroundings, increase the title's screen footprint across tiers, keep the award as the focus, and hold a composed result after the landing. No reference artwork or videos were copied.
- PlaySmith's production workflow: https://playsmith.studio/en/services/spine-animation . Useful distinction between intro, count loop and outro; authored fluid cels and skeletal animation serve different purposes. Existing native Pokémon clips, Spine impact cels and continuous action clocks are retained.

## Implementation

- Larger award typography with dimensional glyph shadows and a brief centre-out title landing at the exact final strike. No text background plaque.
- Every win tier gets a finisher close-up. A persistent victory camera replaces the return to the count-up framing; higher tiers receive a stronger approach.
- Animated-head framing protects the face during the held result. Camera and lighting release with the existing return envelope. Forward and sideways native body motion are tracked so a lunging attack does not compound the camera push or leave the frame.
- Stadium lighting changes on final contact. Four soft volumes originate at the existing two light towers behind the actor; the stands darken, the actor receives stronger rim light, and spectators remain visible.
- Light volumes use four low-poly meshes sharing one material, with no added fullscreen filters. The final lighting pass is prepared before playback to avoid first-use shader/upload work on contact.
- Existing amount count/strike timing, attack sockets, native animation sampling, reduced-motion behavior and settlement are retained.
